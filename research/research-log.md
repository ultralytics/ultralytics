| 28  | 2026-09-22 | bootstrap | **BEDLAM's ground truth does not ship 3D joints.** The v1 training npz carries `imgname, center, scale, pose_cam, pose_world, shape, trans_cam, trans_world, gtkps, cam_int, cam_ext, gender, proj_verts, motion_info, sub` — `gtkps` is **2D**, and root-relative 3D joints require posing SMPL-X from `pose_cam`+`shape`, i.e. the separately gated body model. What is available with no gated asset is still most of what this project needs: `cam_int` gives exact per-frame intrinsics, `trans_cam` gives metric root depth, and `gtkps` gives 2D keypoints. That supervises the 2D half and the **absolute root depth** — the half findings.md identifies as broken — and leaves root-relative depth unsupervised, exactly as 3DPW already leaves the five face joints. Decision surfaced to the human rather than resolved by pulling the body model from an ungated mirror. |
| 29  | 2026-09-22 | inner-loop | **No BEDLAM distribution ships 3D joints, and one earlier claim was wrong.** Checked the real arrays, not the docs: extracted the first npz out of the still-downloading zip by inflating its raw deflate stream (149,943,698 bytes, exact). `gtkps` is (N, 127, 3) with channel 2 identically **1.0** — 2D plus a confidence column, no depth; `proj_verts` (N, 437, 3) is the same. `BEDLAM_hmr4d_support` is no better: GVHMR's own loader reads `smplpose_v2.pth` = `{"pose": (F, 63), "trans": (F, 3), "beta": (10)}` and then calls `make_smplx("supermotion")` to derive joints itself, so that tarball is videos plus cached ViT features plus SMPL-X *parameters*. Joints are derived, never shipped — that is structural to BEDLAM, whose ground truth simply *is* the SMPL-X parameter set. **Correction:** `trans_cam` is not root depth (z median −0.07 m, range −4.47..3.87). The depth is `cam_ext @ trans_world`, which gives z median **10.17 m** (4.70..15.29) — physically sensible, and its reprojection sits 45-66 px from the pelvis keypoint, the offset expected from `J0(shape)` (~0.25 m at 10 m with fx 1312 is ~33 px). So metric root depth is recoverable with no gated asset, to within the pelvis offset (~1-2% of depth); pair it with the mid-hip 2D from `gtkps` to keep the root anatomically consistent with COCO and 3DPW. Root-relative joint depth still needs SMPL-X. |
