# Research Log — YOLOPose-3D

Chronological record of research decisions and actions. Append-only.

| #   | Date       | Type      | Summary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ---------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | 2026-09-16 | bootstrap | Scoped the question against SAM 3D Body (arXiv:2602.15989) and its acceleration follow-up Fast SAM 3D Body (2603.15603). Gap identified: every real-time line is either a server-cost one-stage transformer or an accelerated crop model; none is detector-native. Six hypotheses formed (R0, H1-H5). Literature saved to `literature/survey.md`.                                                   |
| 2   | 2026-09-16 | protocol  | Locked the task design in `design/pose3d-task-design.md` before writing code: `kpt_shape [18, 4]`, channels (x, y, visible, z), joint 17 = root carrying absolute depth, depth encoded to [0, 1]. Metrics locked: MPJPE, PA-MPJPE, AbsRel(Z), delta1(Z), plus ms/person.                                                                                                                             |
| 3   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | H0 supported. Wired the `pose3d` task into the repo and smoke-verified it on ultra1 (CPU): trains 2 epochs with 7 loss terms, validates with the 4 new 3D metrics, reloads as `task=pose3d`, exports to ONNX `(1, 77, 2100)`. Four silent-failure traps found by reading rather than by debugging — see findings.md "Lessons and Constraints". No accuracy claim: the smoke dataset's depth is synthetic. |
| 4   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | Plot/predict paths verified with `plots=True` (they are the default and both smoke runs had disabled them): `Keypoints.has_visible` and `Annotator.kpts` both keyed off `ndim == 3` and silently dropped visibility filtering and the skeleton for a 4-wide keypoint. Fixed at the owner (`>= 3`, and the 18-joint layout recognised as a pose layout). Existing `pose` task re-validated unchanged on coco8-pose. |
| 5   | 2026-09-16 | bootstrap | T1 closed without a download: SAM 3D Body was already installed on **ultra11** (`/data/rick/sam3d`, from the snowboard project) — the HF token on this account returns 403 `GatedRepo` and Shenzhen blocks huggingface.co, so that copy is the only source. Replicated it to ultra9 and ultra15 over the 10.88.0.x LAN at 665 MB/s (the WAN path is 3.7 MB/s, so this mattered). Teacher throughput: **0.77 s/image, ~0.29 s/person** on one RTX 6000D when all of an image's boxes go through `process_one_image` in one call. |
| 6   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | **val2017 pseudo-labelled: 2,346 images, 6,352 persons.** Audit vs COCO GT: 2D OKS mean **0.805**, median **0.893**, 90.6% of persons >0.5, 79.6% >0.75 — the teacher's reprojection agrees closely with human annotation. Root depth median 5.00 m (p5 1.49, p95 13.54, max 23.33); relative depth p1 −0.53, p99 +0.40, absmax 0.95 m. **Zero values hit an encoding bound**, so the ±2 m / 50 m ranges are safe (and the relative one is ~2x looser than it needs to be). |
| 7   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | Dataloader verified on real pseudo-labels: batch keypoints (18, 4), z channel still within [0, 1] after mosaic/affine, root depth median 6.08 m, 82.7% joint visibility. The augmentation carries depth through untouched — which is exactly the H2 hazard made visible: a person mosaic-scaled to half size keeps the same metric depth label. |
| 8   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | train2017 pseudo-labelling launched across 14 idle GPUs (ultra15 x8, ultra9 x5, ultra11 x1), 56,599 images, ETA ~45 min. |
