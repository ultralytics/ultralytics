| 6   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | **val2017 pseudo-labelled: 2,346 images, 6,352 persons.** Audit vs COCO GT: 2D OKS mean **0.805**, median **0.893**, 90.6% of persons >0.5, 79.6% >0.75 — the teacher's reprojection agrees closely with human annotation. | 8   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | train2017 pseudo-labelling launched across 14 idle GPUs (ultra15 x8, ultra9 x5, ultra11 x1), 56,599 images, ETA ~45 min. |
| 9   | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | train2017 pseudo-labelling **complete: 56,599 images**. Three shards had to be restarted — two died at model load on `RemoteDisconnected`, one ran 5 h on a contended GPU producing *nothing* while still looking alive in `ps`. Lesson: check shard liveness by output count, never by process existence. |
| 10  | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | 3DPW test converted to pose3d labels: 4,907 frames (stride 5), 7,097 persons, 3 dropped. Caught a real bug in the process: normalizing the focal as `fx/width` split the 24 sequences into 1.03 and 1.82 clusters, which reads as two cameras but is one camera shot in portrait and landscape. `fx/max(w, h)` — the quantity the validator actually scales by after letterboxing — holds at **1.0218-1.0256**. Using the wrong one would have put a systematic error into every MPJPE. |
| 11  | 2026-09-16 | inner-loop | R0/H2 launched on ultra15 GPUs 0/1/3 after two false starts, both worth recording: (a) `PoseTrainer.__init__` hard-sets the task *before* `BaseTrainer` serializes args, so `args.yaml` recorded `task: pose` and `resume` would have come back as the wrong task — fixed at the owner; (b) the AMP check fetches `yolo26n.pt` over **curl**, which the Shenzhen gh-proxy shim does not cover, so all three hung on a blocked GitHub connection — fixed by placing the weight in `~/weights/` first. First epoch confirms the warm start: B/C open at box_loss ~1.0 and cls_loss ~0.6-0.8 against A's 3.56 and 6.02. |
| 12  | 2026-09-17 | inner-loop | **R0 CONFIRMED, H2 SPLIT.** All three arms finished (100 epochs, ~1.45 h each). R0: 3DPW MPJPE 126.9 -> 113.2 mm, better on every metric on both datasets. H2: AbsRel(Z) improves 24% on teacher agreement but degrades 39% on 3DPW, while delta1(Z) and PA-MPJPE improve on both — the depth-target corruption is real, but scale augmentation is also what buys cross-domain scale robustness. See `experiments/r0-warmstart-h2-augmentation/analysis.md`. |
| 13  | 2026-09-17 | inner-loop | Evaluator bug caught by the real benchmark: PA-MPJPE came out ~620 mm against MPJPE ~120 mm (impossible). `procrustes_align` was fitting over all 17 joints including the five 3DPW cannot annotate, which decode to a phantom point ~2 m off. Now visibility-weighted; PA-MPJPE 79.5/72.8/72.5 mm. Invisible on COCO pseudo-labels, where all 17 joints are populated. |
| 14  | 2026-09-18 | inner-loop | **H2.1 refuted on its metric, supported on its mechanism.** D_scalecomp takes the best 2D mAP (0.7805 pose) and best PA-MPJPE (72.21) of any arm, and in-domain it delivers the promised best-of-both — but 3DPW AbsRel(Z) came out 0.1826, the worst of the four. |
| 15  | 2026-09-18 | inner-loop | The probe that explains it: fitting **one global scalar** per model over 3DPW removes 81% of D's depth error, leaving **AbsRel 0.0350 against B's 0.0893** — D is 2.6x the most accurate arm per person. All three models over-predict depth by ~1.18x, and SAM 3D Body's default FOV (~1.2x image side) over 3DPW's real 1.0239 is **1.17**. The pipeline carries three different focal conventions; the gap is a unit conversion in the data, not a modelling failure. H2's "scale aug buys robustness" is amended: it buys insensitivity to a calibration error we can fix. |
